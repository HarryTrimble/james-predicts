How it’s counted

A result is right when James picked the right outcome: home win, draw or away win. A score is right when both numbers match the full-time score, so every right score is also a right result. Backed the wrong team only counts games where James picked a winner and the other side won; a draw on either side doesn’t count. A team keeps a clean sheet when it concedes nothing, and James predicted one when his score had the other side on zero; scoring 2+ means the team’s own goals, never the match total, so in a 2–1 the home side counts and the away side doesn’t.
